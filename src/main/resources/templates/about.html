Your next salon visit, simply booked.

BookEasy helps customers book salon services and manage their appointments. Salon owners can manage their salon, services, availability and bookings.

BookEasy is a CMPT 276 course project at Simon Fraser University, built for a small course demo with students, teaching assistants and the professor.

Create a BookEasy account

Keep your booking in Google Calendar

Customers can connect Google Calendar from their dashboard. BookEasy then adds each booking to their primary Google Calendar, updates the event when they edit the booking and removes it when they cancel.

Connecting is optional and goes through Google's own permission screen. You can disconnect any time from your dashboard. The privacy policy explains what data is used and how to remove access.
